# LiftCircle user guide

## Getting started

Open the app and tap **Set up my plan**. A short wizard (about 3 minutes) asks for your goal, experience, how many
days you can train, which days, how long a session can be, where you train and what equipment you have, anything to
go easy on, and — only if you want it — nutrition preferences. Age, weight and height are optional and only used for
nutrition targets. You never need to know your one-rep max.

At the end you see your suggested program: the weekly schedule, each session's exercises with sets, rep ranges,
effort and rest, an estimated duration, and *why it fits you*. With 2 or more lifting days you choose the **split**
(for example Full-body A/B or Upper/Lower at 2 days; Full body, Upper/Lower/Full or Push/Pull/Legs at 3 days); the
recommended one is marked. **Change split** switches the structure, **Shuffle exercises** keeps the split and picks
other exercises. You can also swap exercises, edit anything, or accept it. Prefer your own routine? Choose **I already have a program** and build sessions yourself
under **Train → Program**.

**About "reps in reserve" (RIR):** stop each set when you could still do about that many more good reps. Starting
around 2–3 keeps sessions productive without grinding.

**First sessions:** if you didn't enter a comfortable known weight, exercises say *Calibrate* — start light and add
small jumps until a set feels like 2–3 reps left in the tank, then log that as your starting load.

## Logging a workout

- **Start:** Today → *Start workout*, or Train → start any program session, a saved template, *Repeat a workout*, or *Empty workout*.
- **Each set:** type the weight and reps (the numeric keypad opens; a decimal comma works), then tap ✓. What you type is
  saved as you type, even if you don't leave the field. A set needs at least 1 rep (or a time/distance for timed
  moves) to count as done. Values carry into the next set; tap ✓ again to undo. The grey hint shows what you did last time.
- **Swapping an exercise** offers alternatives that fit your equipment and limitations (**Show all exercises** to see
  every option). Switching between rep-based and timed moves rebuilds the sets; finished sets never change.
- **Warm-ups** are always lighter than your working weight. Set your empty bar weight in Settings → Workout.
- **Rest timer** starts automatically after each set: −15 / +15, pause, restart or skip. It's based on the clock, so
  it's correct even if you lock the phone or switch apps. (Whether it can *beep* while locked depends on your browser.)
- **Set types:** tap the set number for warm-up, drop set, timed set, RPE/RIR, copy previous, or delete. Warm-ups don't
  count toward records or volume.
- **Exercise menu (⋯):** swap, move, superset with the next exercise, add a note, or flag **Felt discomfort** — that
  pauses automatic load increases for that exercise and suggests alternatives. It isn't a diagnosis; see a professional
  if pain persists.
- **Quick load change:** the − / + buttons under an exercise adjust all remaining sets by the smallest practical jump.
- **Dumbbells:** enter the weight of *one* dumbbell. **Unilateral moves:** reps are per side. Each exercise shows its rule.
- **Coach cheers:** when you tick a set, a small popup from your coach reacts to it — a new record (with the number),
  the top of your rep range ("weight goes up next time"), the last set of an exercise (it names the next one), the last
  set of the workout, or a few reps short. When the rest timer ends it names your next set, e.g. "Rest's up, Bench press
  set 3: 60 kg × 8. Let's go!" (if the app was in the background, you see it once when you come back). Lines follow your
  coach style (Supportive, Balanced or Strict). If you flagged **Felt discomfort**, it only says caring things. No
  popup for warm-ups or when you undo a set. Tap a popup to dismiss it. It works offline and costs nothing (no AI call).
  Turn it off in Settings → Workout (**Coach cheers during workouts**) or from the workout's ⋮ menu.
- **Pause / resume** the whole workout from the header. **Finish** saves completed sets; unticked sets are dropped.
- Everything saves instantly on your device — closing the app, losing signal, or a failed upload never loses a workout.
  The green **Resume workout** bar brings you back.

After finishing you get a summary with duration, sets, volume (free weights and machines shown separately), and any
**new records**. Next time, the app suggests whether to add weight (after hitting the top of the rep range on every
set twice), hold, or add reps: choose **Apply**, **Keep previous** or **Customize**; **Hide** only hides the tip. If you
didn't log effort (RIR/RPE), the app asks you to confirm before suggesting more. Suggestions never change what you logged.

**History:** Train → History shows a calendar and searchable list. Open a workout to fix a typo, change the date,
add/remove sets, save it as a template, repeat it, or delete it. Charts and records update automatically.

## Activities

Train → **Log activity** for walks, runs, rides, hikes, swims, rowing, sports, classes, yoga, mobility and more:
duration, distance, elevation, heart rate, intensity and notes. Pace/speed is calculated for you. Energy (kcal) is
optional and always an approximation. The **Interval timer** runs work/rest blocks and can log the session afterwards.

## Progress

Consistency vs. your plan, exercise charts (top set, and a clearly labelled *estimated* 1RM), weekly working sets per
muscle, volume with the calculation shown, cardio time/distance/pace, bodyweight with a 7-day average, measurements,
protein consistency (if nutrition is on) and goals you set. Observations are plain facts from your logs.

## Nutrition (optional)

Turn it on from the Nutrition tab: **protein only**, or protein + calories + macros. The default protein target is
1.8 g per kg of a reference weight you choose (current weight, a goal weight, or a custom number) — an adjustable
starting point within a 1.6–2.2 g/kg range, not an exact requirement. Calorie estimates use the Mifflin–St Jeor
equation plus an activity factor and a small goal adjustment, all shown and editable. Log food via quick entries,
recipes, generic foods, your own foods, or saved meals; missing values are treated as *unknown*, not zero.
**Recipes** (24, with vegetarian and dairy-free options) scale by servings and recalculate when you swap ingredients.
**Meal plan** builds a week from your preferences and allergies and balances each day toward your protein target with
portions and protein sides. If a day can't reach it within your restrictions, it shows the gap and practical changes
(allergies are never relaxed); **Balance day** / **Balance all days** re-run it after your edits. In protein-only mode
calories are shown for information only. The **shopping list** adds it all up, sides included. Allergen
filtering uses generic data — always check packaged-food labels.

Under-18s and anyone who indicates pregnancy/breastfeeding or a condition needing individual nutrition advice get
logging and manual targets only, no automatic prescriptions.

## Friends & sharing (needs an account)

- **Create an account:** Settings → Account → *Create account*, confirm the email. If you used the app before signing
  in, you'll be offered to import those records — you see exactly what will be added; nothing is attached silently.
- **Profile:** pick a username and display name; add an avatar and bio if you like. Age, weight, measurements, injuries
  and food logs are never part of your profile. You can turn off *Let people find me by username*.
- **Friends:** search an exact username or share your profile link. Requests can be accepted, declined or cancelled;
  you can remove or block someone at any time.
- **Sharing a workout:** after finishing, choose which details to include (duration, exercise names, weights & reps,
  volume, records), add a caption and up to 4 photos, and choose **Friends** or **Only me**. The post is a snapshot —
  friends never see your full history or anything you didn't select. Photos are resized and their location/camera data
  removed before upload.
- Offline? The post waits on your device as *Waiting to upload* and goes out when you're back online. It isn't visible
  to anyone until then.
- Like, comment, edit your caption, change audience, delete posts, report or block from the post menu.
- **Auto-share** (off by default): Settings → Sharing & privacy. Photos are never added automatically.

## Coaching (needs an account)

- **Look for a coach**: search by town, region or country, or tap *Use my location* (asked only when you tap it; a
  refusal still leaves the manual search). Filter by in person / online, specialty, language and whether the coach is
  taking new clients. Distances appear only when they can actually be calculated. Every listed coach was approved by
  LiftCircle's reviewers; "approved" means platform approval, not a verified credential unless a qualification says so.
- **Request coaching**: a short intake is prefilled from your goals and preferences — remove anything you don't want to
  send. Choose what the coach may see: training history, measurements, nutrition, check-ins. Being friends never gives
  a coach access. Until the coach accepts, they see only the intake and your message.
- **My coach**: messages (queued while offline, sent when you're back online and still allowed), check-ins, and
  program proposals. A proposal shows exactly what changes; nothing happens until you accept it, and accepting adds a
  new version of your plan — finished workouts never change. You can change what you share, end coaching, block or
  report at any time; this takes effect immediately, including for messages still queued on the coach's phone.
  One primary coach at a time: you can request another coach, and when they accept, coaching with the current one ends.
- **Your plan's owner** is shown on Today, Train and the program screen ("Managed by your coach", "AI-suggested").
  You can always make the plan self-managed again from the program screen.
- **AI coach** (if your LiftCircle server has it set up): a short setup, then you agree to what is sent to the AI
  provider (the app names it — OpenAI or Anthropic, depending on the server) and can change or pause that at any time. It explains things using your own records, suggests
  adjustments, and drafts plans — always as a suggestion you preview and apply yourself. If a human coach manages your
  plan, the AI can't replace it without you switching first. Clearing AI history deletes the conversation from
  LiftCircle (suggestions you accepted or declined and daily usage counts are kept); LiftCircle can't delete anything
  on the provider's side.
  It talks like a friendly personal trainer: it greets you by time of day (and your first name, if you share your
  profile), names today's session or wishes you a good rest day, checks in after a missed session and congratulates you
  on new records. Tap a quick reply under the greeting (**Feeling great 💪**, **Bit tired**, **Sore**, **Short on
  time**, **Want to skip today**) to answer it — the greeting itself is made by the app, so it never costs anything.
  Choose a **coach style** in setup or AI coach settings: **Supportive** (gentle, never pushes), **Balanced** (the
  default: encouraging with light accountability) or **Strict** (tough love on excuses). In every style, pain, injury,
  illness, poor sleep or feeling overwhelmed mean rest or an easier plan, and rest days are never "skipping". The coach
  only talks about training, nutrition for your goals, recovery, motivation and using LiftCircle; other requests get a
  short, friendly redirect. It answers briefly (tap **Why this?** for the reasoning). Tell it about a
  one-off change, such as "I can only train twice this week": it picks which of your planned sessions to do, counts
  what you've already done, and offers **Apply to this week**. That only moves or skips sessions for this week (Undo is
  available); your normal schedule comes back next week.
- **Without an account** you can read what human and AI coaching involve and browse approved coaches' public listings;
  requesting, messaging and the AI coach need an account. Unsent messages to your coach are kept as a draft on your
  device.
- **Become a coach**: *Coaching → Become a coach*. Save a draft, submit, and follow the review status. Qualification
  documents are only visible to you and the reviewers.

## Install on your phone

- **iPhone/iPad:** open the site in **Safari** → Share button → **Add to Home Screen** → keep **Open as Web App** on → Add.
- **Android:** Chrome menu (⋮) → **Install app** (or *Add to Home screen*). Some browsers show an Install button in
  Settings → *Install on your phone*.

Launch it from the home-screen icon; it opens full-screen and works offline after the first load. When a new version
is ready you'll see **Update available** — it never reloads in the middle of a workout.

## Backups & your data

- **Settings → Your data → Download full backup** saves a JSON file of all your records (not photo files).
  CSV exports for workouts, activities and body measurements open in Excel/Sheets.
- **Restore** from a backup file: the file is checked completely before anything is written, and the restore either
  finishes fully or changes nothing. The preview shows what's new, identical and different. Records you deleted on this
  device are never brought back. For records that differ, and for each part of Settings & targets, you choose whether
  to keep yours (default) or use the backup's.
- **CSV exports** neutralise text that starts with = + - @ so spreadsheets don't run it as a formula.
- With an account, records sync automatically. If the same record was edited on two devices, you're asked which
  version to keep (or keep both) — nothing is silently lost.
- Browser data can be cleared by you or the browser. Installing the app and tapping *Ask the browser to keep my data*
  helps; regular backups or an account are the safe options.
- **Sign out** first backs up what it can; you decide whether to keep a copy on the device. **Delete account** removes
  everything from the server and the device.
- **Demo data:** Settings → *Try with demo data* opens a separate, clearly labelled sample dataset. *Exit demo* deletes it.
