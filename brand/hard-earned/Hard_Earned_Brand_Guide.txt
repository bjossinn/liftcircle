BRAND GUIDE

BRAND FOUNDATION

Hard Earned is a fitness, workout and weight tracking app built around consistency and earned progress. It gives the work someone puts into training a clear, useful record. The experience should make the next action obvious and the history easy to understand.

The brand in one view

Element | Decision
Public name | Hard Earned
Wordmark | HARD EARNED
Primary line | Your work, made visible.
Product descriptor | Workout and weight tracker
Brand promise | Make the work easier to record, understand and build on.
Personality | Steady, capable, encouraging and precise.
Visual identity | The original H-like dumbbell on charcoal, with a vivid ember corner detail.

Positioning

For people who want a clearer picture of their training, Hard Earned turns logged workouts and weight entries into a history they can use. Its identity respects effort; its experience helps people record a session, review what happened and keep going.

What the name means

Hard Earned recognizes the time and attention a person gives to training. It does not ask people to earn their worth, food, rest or belonging. The name already carries intensity, so the interface should feel composed and welcoming. A first workout deserves the same clear experience as a long training history.

Who it serves

Welcome new lifters, experienced trainees, people returning after a break and people fitting exercise around a busy life. Support varied goals without assuming weight loss, competition, daily training or public sharing. Human coach and client areas should follow the same principles when those features are present.

Four product principles

• Make the next action clear. Logging should require little explanation.
• Show useful evidence. Describe progress using the data actually recorded.
• Respect the routine. Rest, adjusted plans and returning after a gap belong in training.
• Let the work lead. Use restrained visuals and specific language.


THE LOGO AND APP ICON

The approved mark is the first concept from the original ten-logo exploration: a horizontal off-white dumbbell with two tall inner plates and two shorter outer plates. The shaft and tall plates form a subtle H. A small vivid ember folded-corner detail sits at the upper right of the charcoal field.

Full bleed background and internal space

The app icon background must reach every edge of the square. Remove the surrounding presentation canvas, captions and transparent outer margin. Keep space between the dumbbell and the icon edges so the symbol remains clear after platform masking. Full bleed describes the background, not a symbol stretched to the edges.

The corrected supplied master is an opaque, square raster. Its corners are not pre-rounded. Google Play applies its own masking and shadow; the store asset should remain a full square. Apple and native launchers should receive assets through the project’s supported icon pipeline. [1, 2]

Asset roles

Asset | Use
1024 × 1024 PNG | Flattened default icon master for the app asset pipeline.
512 × 512 Play Store PNG | 32-bit sRGB PNG with opaque alpha; under 1024 KB. [2]
512 and 192 px PWA PNGs | Standard web app install icons with purpose any.
512 px maskable PNG | Separate manifest entry with purpose maskable; essential mark checked against the central safe circle. [3]
180 px Apple touch icon | Web app touch icon. Native assets use the native build pipeline.
32 and 16 px favicons | Browser tab sizes; verify visual recognition at actual size.

A PWA maskable icon protects essential artwork inside a central circle with radius 40% of the icon width. The orange corner is secondary and may be clipped by a platform mask. Native Android adaptive foreground/background layers have their own requirements and are not supplied as finished layers in this kit. [3]

Logo use beyond launchers

Keep a clear area around the mark at least equal to one tall inner plate’s width when it appears beside other content. This is a brand layout rule, not extra blank pixels to add around a launcher image. Use a single mark, preserve its proportions, and keep it separate from the tagline. The symbol contains no lettering.

Do not replace the approved mark with a joined HE monogram, tally, staircase, arrow or a newly drawn H. Do not stretch, add glossy depth, use a decorative border or put the full app name inside the icon. For a future monochrome vector, preserve the same four-plate silhouette and review the result at small sizes. The supplied logo files are raster assets, not editable vector artwork.


COLOUR DIRECTION IN THE APP

Charcoal, chalk and vivid ember are the core identity. Dark is the flagship presentation. If the product already offers light mode, keep it and make it feel like the same brand. Preserve the person’s saved theme preference.

Colour | Hex | Primary role
Charcoal | #17191B | App canvas, ink on orange, icon background
Chalk | #F3EFE7 | Main dark-theme text, light-theme canvas
Ember | #FF694F | Primary actions and focused progress emphasis

Use large areas of neutral colour and reserve ember for the next action, selected navigation and a small number of meaningful highlights. An approximate 80–90% neutral visual balance is a composition guide, not a pixel quota. A screen with many orange controls loses hierarchy.

[Illustration: Illustrative UI only. Example data shows how the same brand works in dark and light modes; this is not a screenshot of the current app.]

A practical screen recipe

Use a charcoal canvas, slightly lighter cards, chalk headings, legible grey supporting text and one prominent ember primary action. Keep input boundaries visible. In light mode, use a chalk canvas, white cards, charcoal text and the same ember action with a charcoal outline. Use dark ember ink for orange links on light surfaces.

The canonical accent is the original vivid ember #FF694F. The later muted burnt-orange exploration is not part of this identity. Keep semantic success, warning, error and information colours available for their specific meanings.

Rules that preserve clarity

• Use primary text for exercise names, entered values and important totals. Keep supporting and muted text fully opaque.
• Use the subtle divider only for decoration or grouping already clear from layout. Use the control boundary where the border identifies an input or selectable control.
• Use success for saved or completed states, error for failures and invalid fields, warning for needed attention, and information for neutral guidance. Pair every status colour with visible words or a recognisable icon.
• In light mode, use #A93623 for accent text and meaningful icons. Raw #FF694F is too light for these roles on the specified light surfaces.

This palette is a design specification with measured solid-colour pairs. It is not a claim that an uninspected app meets every accessibility requirement. Check real rendered screens, including overlays and third-party components. [4, 5]

Ratios were computed from opaque sRGB values using WCAG relative luminance. The accompanying matrix evaluates 172 pair/use entries: 152 approved uses all pass their assigned thresholds; the other entries identify decorative uses, light fills needing a boundary, and rejected uses. Values above are rounded for display only. [6]

Normal text needs 4.5:1 under WCAG AA; qualifying large text needs 3:1. Required control/state cues and meaningful graphics need 3:1 against adjacent colours. Decorative borders have different roles from functional boundaries. This system generally keeps functional text above 4.5:1 regardless of size. [4, 5]

Do not dim an entire disabled control with opacity. Explain a disabled action when the cause is unclear. Keep focus visible, unclipped and clear of sticky controls; the 3 px treatment is a product design choice, not a claim that WCAG AA prescribes that dimension. [7]


TYPOGRAPHY AND LAYOUT

Type system

Use Inter for functional UI, exercise names and numbers. Use Barlow Semi Condensed 700 for major brand headings and the wordmark when a second family is justified. All-Inter is an approved practical fallback. Keep body copy at normal width and reserve all caps for the wordmark or occasional short category labels.

Role | Size and line height | Weight
Body and exercise name | 16 / 24 px | 400–500
Button and field label | 16 / 20–24 px | 600
Supporting text | 14 / 20 px | 400–500
Screen heading | 28 / about 32 px | 700
Primary workout statistic | 40 / 44 px | 700, tabular numerals
Campaign heading | 40–64 px / 1.05–1.1 | 700

These sizes are visual references at a 16 px default root. Implement scalable units and allow user text enlargement. Enable lining, tabular numerals for timers, sets and tables so values remain aligned. Show the unit beside the value. Inter supports tabular number features; both recommended font projects publish SIL Open Font License 1.1 terms. [8, 9]

Self-host fonts, retain their licences and use a system sans-serif fallback. Font binaries are not bundled. Verify glyph coverage, including Æfing, Þyngd, Endurtekningar and 102,5 kg.

Spacing and shapes

Use a 4 px grid: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48 and 64. Start with 16–20 px mobile side padding, 20–24 px section gaps and 12–16 px inside cards. App layouts need comfortable padding.

Element | Design target
Small radius | 8 px
Control radius | 12 px
Card radius | 16 px
Dialog radius | 24 px
Workout target box | At least 48 × 48 CSS px
Set row | At least 56 px high

The 48 px target is a chosen product standard. WCAG 2.2 AA uses a 24 × 24 CSS px target criterion with specific spacing and other exceptions. Give weight, reps and completion separate touch areas. [10]

Depth and motion

Use restrained shadows, 120 ms state-colour changes and 160–200 ms panel transitions. Keep focus immediate. Respect reduced motion and make completion clear after animation ends. Avoid bouncing totals, glossy gradients and texture behind data. [11]


APPLYING THE BRAND TO COMPONENTS

Component | Treatment
Navigation | Neutral background; selected item uses accent ink plus a shape, indicator or label state. Keep inactive labels readable.
Primary action | One dominant action per section. Ember fill, charcoal label, explicit verb such as Log set or Save workout.
Cards | Charcoal surface in dark mode, white in light. Use a heading, useful spacing and a subtle divider. Do not make every panel orange.
Inputs | Persistent labels, clear units, readable entered values, visible boundaries, specific validation. Avoid placeholder-only labels.
Workout rows | Align weight, reps and completion. Keep units visible, preserve entered values, and make editing a completed set understandable.

Feedback | Brief acknowledgement of the actual result. Saved means persisted in the location claimed; distinguish local save from completed sync.
Badges and chips | Short labels with semantic meaning. Do not make every numeric improvement a reward badge.

Charts and progress

A simple trend uses one ember series in dark mode or dark ember ink in light mode. Label the metric, unit, time range and comparison. Keep exercise load distinct from body weight. A larger or smaller body-weight value is not automatically better; describe the change neutrally and respect the person’s goal.

The supplied checks compare each series with its plot background, not every series with every other series. Colour alone must not carry selection, status or progress meaning. [12]

Imagery

Prefer actual product screens and honest training context. If photography is used, favour natural lighting, varied experience levels and ordinary training spaces. Avoid physique promises, aggressive stock poses and busy imagery behind controls. The icon remains the single visual signature; repeated dumbbell decorations add little value.


VOICE AND LANGUAGE

Write like a helpful training partner who respects the person’s time. Be clear before being motivational. Use concrete verbs and acknowledge the event that actually happened. The product should sound steady during a good session, a difficult session and a return after a break.

Principle | Use | Avoid
Clear | Log your first workout. | Unlock your ultimate potential.
Specific | Workout saved. | You destroyed that workout!
Supportive | Ready for your next session? | You fell behind.
Useful | Compare with your last session. | Next-level insights.
Honest | We couldn’t save this workout. Try again. | An unexplained Oops!
Respectful | Choose your weekly schedule. | No excuses.

Operating rules

• Keep action labels plain: Save, Edit, Continue, Log workout and Review history.
• Use the primary motto at selected brand moments, not beneath every card.
• Mention a personal record only when a defined comparison supports it.
• Avoid punishment, pain worship, food guilt, body insults and claims that rest erases progress.
• Use sentence case and restrained punctuation. Avoid em dashes in brand copy and unnecessary exclamation marks.
• Do not promise coaches, integrations, automated feedback or offline behaviour that the current product does not provide.

Localization

Keep Hard Earned unchanged across languages. Use the approved English brand line until another version has been deliberately approved. Translate interface actions naturally and maintain a shared glossary. For Icelandic, examples include Skrá æfingu, Æfingasaga, Vista breytingar and Hætta við. Have locale copy reviewed where terms could confuse a session with a movement.

Localize dates, decimals, plurals and unit labels. Respect kg/lb settings and existing conversion logic. Make room for longer labels and larger text. Avoid the Icelandic word sameinar in marketing copy. Keep all user-facing strings in the project’s localization system where one exists.

Context makes the copy true

Show “Workout saved” only when the application has actually completed the save it promises. If offline persistence exists, distinguish “Saved on this device” from “Synced.” Do not promise that input is safe unless it is. Offer Undo only when deletion is reversible.

Do not judge weight changes as good or bad by default. Use neutral wording such as “Change since your last entry” with the measured value and date. Do not imply that a day without a log is a failed day.

Notifications when implemented

Workout reminder: “Your workout reminder. Ready for a session?” Use only for a schedule the person has enabled. A quiet reminder is enough; avoid guilt or repeated demands.

Saved summary: “Your workout is saved. Review the session when you’re ready.” Send only if this event and notification type exist. Brand the sender and title consistently, but keep the message useful.

Accessibility labels should identify the action and object, such as “Edit bench press set 2.” A generic “button” label or an unexplained colour name is insufficient. Keep screen-reader names and visible wording aligned.


COACHING AND FEATURE DEPENDENT COPY

This chapter supplies naming and tone for features only if the repository contains them. It does not authorize new product features, new subscriptions, a new coach marketplace or altered training logic.

AI assistance

Use Training assistant for AI. Reserve Coach for a human. Keep that distinction visible. A branded assistant can be calm, context-aware and concise without presenting its suggestions as instructions from a human trainer.

When current onboarding answers are available to the assistant, use them instead of asking the person to repeat goals, experience and weekly availability. Lead with a concrete suggestion and a short reason. If essential information is missing, ask one useful question. Preserve existing approved training and safety behaviour during the rebrand.

Opening pattern: “Hi [name]. Based on your [number]-day schedule, here’s a suggested plan for this week: [short plan].” Use this only after the system has actually read the schedule and produced a compatible suggestion. Never ship the bracketed placeholders.

Suggested actions: “Adjust the plan” and “Explain this choice.” Do not promise diagnosis, injury treatment, form assessment or live feedback unless a real, supported service provides it.

COMPLETE NAME REPLACEMENT

Replace every current LiftCircle brand reference with Hard Earned. This includes hidden product surfaces as well as the main interface. The completion standard is zero unresolved current user-facing references to the old brand.

Audit every surface that exists

• Navigation, authentication, dashboard, workouts, weight tracking, history, settings, onboarding, empty/loading/error states, dialogs and account areas.
• Human coach, client and admin views; AI names, system instructions, greetings, examples and canned responses.
• Browser title, page metadata, structured data, Open Graph and social cards, favicons, touch icons, PWA name and short_name, install banners and launch screens.
• Notifications, push payload templates, transactional email content and sender display names, export headings, generated filenames and downloadable reports.
• All locale files, accessibility labels, image alt text, screenshots and text baked into image assets.
• Native display names, store-listing content tracked in the repo, documentation, examples, tests, fixtures, package descriptions and active design assets.

Search thoroughly

Use a case-insensitive search for joined, spaced, hyphenated, underscored and casing variants: LiftCircle, Lift Circle, liftcircle, lift-circle and lift_circle. A useful ripgrep pattern is lift[[:space:]_-]*circle. Include appropriate hidden configuration and inspect filenames, not only text contents. Exclude .git, dependencies and generated caches from the main source scan; review build outputs separately if applicable.

Define the scan scope and counting method before editing. Record baseline occurrences and matching files, then use the same scope after the work. Inspect image assets visually because a text search cannot read all embedded branding.

Protect continuity during the rename

Rename safe internal symbols and file names with their imports and references. Preserve stored workouts, account identities, authentication, billing, saved settings, local/offline data and live integration contracts. A display name can change without changing an immutable bundle ID, production project ID or database key.

Where an old technical key needs to change, implement and verify a backward-compatible migration. Do not guess replacement domains, support addresses, OAuth callbacks, credentials or legal entity names. Change display text and owned assets now; record exact external changes still required. An unresolved visible old sender or domain remains an explicit launch blocker, not a silent exception.

Residual reference register

Every remaining match needs a file or system location, classification, reason, visible impact and migration requirement. Only exact compatibility references or deliberate historical records belong on the allowlist. Do not rewrite immutable user content or Git history. Avoid broad exclusions that hide current branding.

A transitional “LiftCircle is now Hard Earned” announcement is optional and must be deliberately approved for a defined placement and duration. It is not default ongoing product copy.

How to interpret the checks

Contrast calculations use solid sRGB foreground and background values at full opacity. There are 172 evaluated pair/use entries, not 172 distinct colour pairs. Passing the selected pairs does not audit every real rendered screen or establish whole-app accessibility conformance. App integration must also provide semantics, keyboard support, meaningful labels, correct state handling and readable responsive layouts.
